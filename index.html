How to play
  • 12
    12 set shots, 12 different categories. One answer per question, no second go. Any answer that fits is a valid kick.
  • m
    Rare answers also kick further. Common ones only travel 25–40m, so from long range or a tight angle they drift for a behind or fall short.
  • ✦
    Find a Wizardry-tier answer and you snap one around your body from anywhere. It's always a goal, so you bank the full 100.
  • pts
    Rarer answer, more points. Each answer is worth 10, 30, 60, 85 or 100 by rarity. Kick a goal to bank all of it, a behind banks half, a miss banks nothing.
  • 🔥
    Hot streak. Kick rare goals in a row (Drop punt or rarer) and your 2nd earns ×1.25, then ×1.5 after that. A basic answer pauses the streak. A behind or miss resets it.
  • 📅
    Daily streak. Play today's match on back-to-back days for up to +50% lifetime points.
  • ✦
    Lifetime points unlock the Locker. New balls and kick trails, plus a Spellbook of every deep cut you find.
  • vs
    Beat the opposition. Their score for each quarter goes up at the first bounce, so you know exactly what you need.
  • ∑
    Every goal goes on your career tally. Climb past Hudson, Lloyd and Buddy all the way to Plugger's record of 1,360.
  • 30
    30-second shot clock, same as the real thing. Run out and you're smothered.

Rarity is our estimate of how often footy fans would name each answer, not live player data. A new match drops every day at 10am, Melbourne time.
